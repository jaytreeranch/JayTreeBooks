Privacy Policy

JayTree Social Poster

Effective: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JayTree Books handles information used by JayTree Social Poster, a private automation tool that publishes approved promotional content to connected social-media accounts.

Information the app accesses

When JayTree Social Poster is authorized with Google, it requests the YouTube upload permission required to upload videos to the authorized JayTree Books YouTube channel. Google may provide OAuth credentials, including access and refresh tokens, so the app can perform authorized uploads.

How information is used

Google account authorization and OAuth credentials are used only to authenticate the connected account and perform publishing functions requested or scheduled by JayTree Books. JayTree Social Poster does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored in private local or private automation environments used by JayTree Books. They are not intentionally exposed in public website files or public source-code repositories. Reasonable technical controls are used to restrict access to these credentials.

Sharing of information

JayTree Books does not sell Google user data or OAuth credentials. Information is not shared with third parties except as necessary for the connected platform to provide the requested publishing functionality, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials are retained only while needed for the automation to remain connected. Access can be removed by revoking JayTree Social Poster from the Google Account security settings. Stored credentials may also be deleted from the private automation environment at any time.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JayTree Social Poster's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy or JayTree Social Poster may be sent to jaytreebooks@gmail.com.
