What kind of mystery are you looking for?

Some mystery readers want a cold case and a town full of secrets. Others want a psychological thriller where memory and reality cannot be trusted. Some want the supernatural just close enough to make every clue feel dangerous. JayTree Books spans those corners of the mystery genre, with each title built around a different central question rather than the same case in a different setting.

Small-Town • Cold Case • Supernatural Mystery

The Hollow Bell

A twenty-year disappearance. A body beneath the ice. A bell that should not be ringing.

In Millbrook Falls, journalist Claire Renner returns to the mystery of her missing sister while Sheriff Del Hutchins is pulled into a case the town never truly escaped. The investigation moves through old records, local history, drownings, and a legend that refuses to stay in the past.

Try this if you like: atmospheric small-town mysteries, cold cases, missing-person investigations, winter settings, journalists and sheriffs working a case, and supernatural elements woven into a grounded mystery.

Supernatural • Memory • Disappearance Mystery

The Hollow Year

In Amity Hollow, one person is erased from every living memory each October.

Enid begins with a feeling that someone should be there, a second mug, a phone number, and a brother she knows she has but cannot prove exists. Set in a fog-bound Maine town, the mystery turns absence itself into evidence as memory, family, and reality begin to come apart.

Try this if you like: supernatural mystery books, missing-person stories with an unusual premise, memory mysteries, eerie Maine settings, reality-bending suspense, and slow-building dread.

Psychological • Memory • Reality-Bending Suspense

Second Draft

What if rehearsing the truth could change the real thing?

Nora runs Second Draft, a private service where people rehearse the conversations they are afraid to have. Then details from those rehearsals begin appearing in the real world. A client’s story becomes harder to separate from Nora’s own life, and certainty becomes the first thing she loses.

Try this if you like: psychological mystery thrillers, unreliable reality, memory and identity puzzles, unsettling slow-burn suspense, and mysteries where the reader has to question what is actually happening.

Archives • Altered Records • Conspiracy Mystery

The Correction

Some records should not be changed. Some truths should not be forgotten.

In Averill Falls, the archive is where the town keeps what it has decided is true. A corrector quietly repairs errors in obituaries and public records until a correction slip points toward something much larger than a typo. The deeper the record is examined, the less trustworthy the official version becomes.

Try this if you like: archival mysteries, hidden records, small-town conspiracies, historical documents, altered evidence, institutional secrets, and psychological suspense built around who controls the truth.

Family Secrets • Homecoming • Psychological Mystery

The Absconding

A death by the hives brings Del home. The explanation does not fit.

Del works restoring fragile historical documents when a call pulls her back to the family home: her mother has been found dead near the beehives before dawn. What begins as grief and practical questions opens into a darker mystery involving old history, buried truths, and the things families preserve by refusing to name them.

Try this if you like: family-secret mysteries, suspicious deaths, rural atmosphere, archival clues, homecoming suspense, beekeeping settings, and psychological mystery thrillers that uncover the past layer by layer.
