The Story

A shorter visit to Millbrook Falls. The same uneasy bell.

In a town with secrets it would prefer to keep, an old bell becomes an unsettling reminder of what has been buried. The novella delivers a shorter version of this mystery for readers looking for a more compact read.

Prefer the expanded story? The full-length novel is a separate edition with its own Amazon listing. These are different editions of a related mystery—not a Book 1 and Book 2 reading sequence.
