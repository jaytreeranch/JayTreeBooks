About the Mystery

What if everyone forgot someone you know exists?

In Amity Hollow, Maine, Enid comes home from a night shift to a warm coffee pot she does not remember making and two mugs drying beside the sink. Then a name arrives with the force of certainty: Caleb, her brother. She can remember his habits, his work, even a scar on his hand—but the world around her offers no proof that he belongs in it.

The Hollow Year is a supernatural disappearance mystery about erased memory, impossible absence, family, and the evidence left behind when a person vanishes from more than a place. It is built for readers who like eerie small towns, Maine atmosphere, reality-bending suspense, slow-building dread, and mysteries where memory itself becomes part of the crime scene.
