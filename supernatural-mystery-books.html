The Uncanny as Evidence

What makes these supernatural mysteries different?

The supernatural element is not decoration. It changes what can count as a clue. In The Hollow Year, absence and failed memory become evidence that someone has been erased. In The Hollow Bell, a decades-old disappearance and a body beneath the ice collide with a local legend that refuses to remain only a story.

The Hollow Year

In Amity Hollow, one person is erased from every living memory each October.

Enid knows someone is missing even when nobody else remembers him. A second mug, a phone number, and the shape of an absence become clues in a mystery where memory itself cannot be trusted.

The mechanism: proof of a forgotten person has to survive after the town's living memory does not.

The Hollow Bell

A twenty-year disappearance. A body beneath the ice. A bell that should not be ringing.

Journalist Claire Renner returns to Millbrook Falls while Sheriff Del Hutchins investigates a discovery that reopens a case the town never escaped. The evidence is physical; the bell is harder to explain.

The mechanism: a grounded cold-case investigation keeps colliding with a local legend that may be more than folklore.
