JayTree Social Poster

JayTree Social Poster is a private publishing automation tool used by JayTree Books to prepare and publish promotional content across supported social-media platforms.

What the app does

The app helps JayTree Books automate approved social posts, including uploading videos to the JayTree Books YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public consumer service and does not allow outside users to create accounts.

Google / YouTube access

When connected to Google, JayTree Social Poster requests the YouTube upload permission needed to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel. The application uses this access only for the publishing functions initiated or scheduled by JayTree Books.

Privacy

For details about information handled by the app, token storage, data sharing, retention, and revoking Google access, read the JayTree Social Poster Privacy Policy.
