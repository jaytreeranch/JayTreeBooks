Old Evidence • New Questions

Why cold cases make powerful mysteries.

A cold case has two timelines: what happened, and everything people have believed since. That gap gives old records, local memory, photographs, physical evidence, and changing testimony unusual weight. JayTree mysteries use those layers differently rather than treating every old secret as the same puzzle.
