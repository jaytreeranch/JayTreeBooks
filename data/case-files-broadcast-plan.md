# JayTree Case Files — 5-Week Broadcast Plan

Sender: **JayTree Books <casefiles@jaytreebooks.com>**

Primary goal: keep subscribers engaged with mysteries, clues, first chapters, trailers, and Kindle Unlimited reading without making the list feel like a constant sales feed.

- **Tuesday — Case File clue/question:** engagement-first; invite the reader to make a choice, notice a detail, or think like an investigator.
- **Thursday — Featured mystery:** spotlight the week's book with one strong hook plus first-chapter/trailer/KU CTA.
- **Saturday — Weekend case:** Mystery Challenge reminder or a lighter Kindle Unlimited CTA.

Use the same weekly title rotation as the JayTree social campaign:

# Repeat cycle

After Week 5, restart with **The Hollow Bell** and refresh the Tuesday/Saturday mystery questions so repeat subscribers do not receive identical copy.

## Broadcast rules

1. Use **casefiles@jaytreebooks.com** as the From address.
2. Keep A/B testing off until the list has enough subscribers for a meaningful split.
3. Send only to confirmed subscribers.
4. Keep the Tuesday email engagement-first and avoid a hard sales pitch.
5. Thursday may use the strongest book CTA because it is the featured-book day.
6. Saturday should feel like a weekend mystery invitation, not a second Thursday sales email.
7. Keep one primary CTA per email when possible.
8. Preserve Kit's unsubscribe/profile footer.
9. Use the current Mystery Challenge link whenever a new case replaces the existing one.
10. Review open/click performance after each five-week cycle and adjust subject lines and CTA placement.
