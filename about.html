About JayTree Books

Stories built around a mystery you can name.

JayTree Books is an independent mystery publisher and story studio creating dark mysteries, psychological thrillers, supernatural secrets, cinematic trailers, first-chapter previews, and interactive Mystery Challenges. Each title is built around a distinct central mechanism rather than repeating the same mystery in a different setting.
